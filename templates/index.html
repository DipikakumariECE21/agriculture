The problem

Farmers don't lack effort. They lack an early second opinion.

Disease is usually identified by eye. Experienced growers spot a lot — but inspection is slow, subjective, and expert help is often a long ride away. Late or wrong identification leads to delayed treatment, wasted pesticide and infection that spreads row by row.

What the model sees

Not a picture. A set of measurements.

  • Segmentation

    Leaf tissue is separated from soil, sky and hands using HSV colour thresholds and a smoothed mask.

  • Lesion map

    Brown, rust-orange, yellow, white and necrotic pixels inside the leaf are isolated and measured.

  • Spot statistics

    Connected-component labelling counts spots and sizes the largest — small-and-many reads differently from large-and-few.

  • Quality gate

    Blur and exposure are checked first. A bad photo gets a warning, not a confident wrong answer.
