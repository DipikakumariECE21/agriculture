For a reliable result

  1. One leaf, filling the frame. Overlapping leaves confuse segmentation.
  2. Daylight, no glare. Shade or harsh sun both shift colours.
  3. Hold steady. Tap to focus — blurred photos are flagged.
  4. Check the underside too. Rust and mildew often start there.
