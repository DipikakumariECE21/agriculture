Testing

How we judge it.

Performance is measured with accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score and a confusion matrix on images the model has never seen. The interface is tested against four behaviours:

  • Precision of the scans flagged as a disease, how many really were
  • Recall of the diseased leaves, how many were caught
  • F1 the balance of the two
